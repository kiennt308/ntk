### Who I Am
I am **Kien Nguyen**, a Senior DevOps / Cloud / SRE Engineer with over 6 years of experience architecting, scaling, and automating complex cloud infrastructure networks. My engineering practice centers around treating infrastructure as software, building self-healing container platforms, and establishing end-to-end observability systems.

### What I Do
Currently, I work with high-growth organizations to modernise their delivery systems and runtime planes:
* **Cloud Architecture:** Designing multi-region, multi-account AWS environments that scale securely.
* **Platform Engineering:** Standardizing developer environments and provisioning pipelines to increase development velocity.
* **Site Reliability Engineering (SRE):** Applying software solutions to operations, designing SLAs/SLOs, and optimizing application availability and performance.

### Technical Interests & Domain Focus
My technical focus shifts between industrial cloud platforms and academic research:
* **Infrastructure as Code (IaC):** Modular Terraform architectures, Terragrunt multi-env control, and Compliance-as-Code.
* **Cloud-Native Foundations:** Service mesh layers, custom Kubernetes controllers, and bare-metal cluster networking.
* **Academic Research:** Exploring intersections of artificial intelligence and neuroscience—specifically *AI / EEG Multimodal Emotion Recognition*.

### Engineering Philosophy
* **Automate Everything:** If a task needs to be performed more than twice, it deserves a tested script or pipeline.
* **Simplicity by Design:** Complex pipelines break easily. Build modular, observable, and simple systems.
* **Documentation is Code:** Systems that are not documented do not exist. Good engineering means maintaining clear runbooks and technical guidelines.
* **Safety First:** Implement least-privilege policies, security gates in CI/CD, and continuous vulnerability scanning at all layers.
