## Abstract
Smart wearables, equipped with sensors monitoring physiological parameters, are becoming an integral part of our life. In this work, we investigate the possibility of utilizing such wearables to recognize emotions in the wild. In most reviewed papers, the authors apply a similar procedure consisting of participant recruitment, stimuli preparation and annotation, signal collection and processing, self-assessment, and machine learning model learning and validation. Besides, we identified seven emotion recognition scenarios and analyzed the transition from psychological models to machine learning tasks.
