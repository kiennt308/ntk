## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Formulation
- **Objective**: Multimodal Latent Emotion Recognition from Micro-expression and Physiological Signals.
- **Target Challenges**: Capturing long-range temporal dependencies in continuous physiological signals, modeling cross-modal semantic interactions between asynchronous biosignals, and managing quadratic attention complexity.
- **Key Proposition**: Utilizing advanced Transformer architectures, Cross-Modal Multi-Head Attention, and Hierarchical Sequence Transformers for robust multimodal affective computing.

## 2. Core Methodology & Architectural Framework
- **Transformer Architecture**: Employs Cross-Attention Transformers, Multimodal Bottleneck Transformers (MBT), Perceiver/Perceiver IO architectures, or Linear/State-Space Attention (Mamba/S4).
- **Modality Integration**: Integrates EEG, ECG, PPG, EDA, facial expressions, eye-tracking, and acoustic speech features through pairwise or shared cross-modal attention maps.
- **Affective Target**: Continuous valence-arousal regression and categorical emotion classification on SEED, DEAP, CASE, AMIGOS, and MuSe benchmarks.

## 3. Key Findings & Benchmark Performance
- Achieves superior performance in tracking continuous emotional dynamics over long trial durations compared to traditional RNNs/LSTMs.
- Reveals interpretable cross-modal attention weights indicating when peripheral signals compensate for ambiguous EEG signals.

## 4. Alignment with PhD Research (MMB-EmotionNet)
- **Direct Application**: Serves as the blueprint for the **Cross-Modal Attention Fusion Core** in MMB-EmotionNet to exchange affective cues between EEG and peripheral branches.
- **Extension**: Combines cross-modal self-attention with multi-task loss functions to predict multidimensional emotional coordinates simultaneously.
